# Partner Bug Report Template

Use this when the tool seems wrong, confusing, broken, or risky.

Do **not** paste the user's exact conversation.
Do **not** include names, addresses, phone numbers, case numbers, or other
private details.

Paraphrase instead.

---

## Five questions

1. Which button did they tap?
   - `Understand a letter or form`
   - `Write something`
   - `Think it through`
   - `Figure out what to do next`
   - `Explain something like I'm new to it`
   - `Prepare for something hard`
   - `Am I being unreasonable`
   - `Something I'm embarrassed to ask`
   - `Type your own`
   - `Talk instead`

2. What language was the conversation in?

3. What was the user trying to do, in your words?

4. What did the model say, in your paraphrase?
   - Do not paste the actual reply.

5. What felt wrong?
   - wrong facts
   - missed the point
   - unsafe advice
   - too vague
   - too harsh
   - too soft
   - broken UI / technical problem
   - other

Optional:

- Anything else useful for the builder?

---

## Good report example

> Button: Understand a letter or form
>
> Language: English
>
> User goal: They got a benefits recertification notice and wanted to know
> whether they would lose food assistance if they missed the deadline.
>
> Model reply, paraphrased: It explained the notice in general terms but did
> not clearly answer what the deadline meant or what to do first.
>
> What felt wrong: Too vague. It did not give a concrete first step.

---

## Bad report example

> The AI was weird.

That is too hard to act on.

---

## Submission rule

Send the report by email or put it into the shared bug-report form once that
exists.

The goal is enough detail to reproduce the issue with a synthetic prompt,
without collecting the user's real content.
