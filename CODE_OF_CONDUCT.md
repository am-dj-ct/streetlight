# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, or experience level.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but prioritize clarity and safety.
- Focus critique on ideas and implementation, not people.
- Help keep discussions accessible and welcoming.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, threats, or discrimination.
- Trolling, personal attacks, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close threads, reject contributions,
or block participants whose behavior violates this Code of Conduct.

Reports can be sent to:
- `jesse@balancedlivingtherapy.com`

All reports will be reviewed and handled in good faith, with as much privacy as
practical.
